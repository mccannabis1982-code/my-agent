The Verified Oracle

Ask a question about a hard moment. The answer is built only from verified words the Stoics actually wrote — every line cited. If no verified words answer it, the Oracle says so, and stays silent.

How this works — and why it never invents

Most “talk to Marcus” products let an AI improvise in a philosopher’s voice. This one does the opposite.

  1. A fixed bank of 36 quotations. Every line below was copied word for word from a named public-domain translation and checked against the source text. There is nothing else in the bank — no modern paraphrases, no summaries, no invented lines.
  2. Matching, not generating. Your question is matched to the quotes by topic keywords (anger, grief, waiting, money, fear, control, failure, other people, death, work, and more). The answer is a small collage of the best-matching quotes, up to three. Nothing is written fresh.
  3. Every line is cited. Each quote shows its author, work, and book, chapter, or letter — so you can check it yourself.
  4. Silence over invention. If no quote in the bank answers your question, the Oracle tells you plainly and offers nothing instead. That honesty is the product.

What the Oracle refuses to invent: the well-known line “You have power over your mind, not outside events” is a modern summary of Meditations 8.47, not a sentence Marcus Aurelius wrote — so it is not in the bank, and the Oracle will never quote it as his words. The genuine passage (8.47) is in the bank instead.
