How to Use This Planner

This planner is undated, so you can start it on any week, on any shift pattern. Print the pages you need and reuse them week after week.

A simple weekly routine

  1. Before the week: fill in the rotating-schedule calendar (pages 10–13) with your shifts.
  2. Each work day: use the sleep-window planner (page 3) and the before-shift checklist (page 8).
  3. After each shift: run the after-shift reset checklist (page 9) before you try to sleep.
  4. During the shift: note meals and caffeine on pages 5–6, and energy on pages 14–15.
  5. End of the week: do the weekly review (page 16) and adjust next week's sleep window.

Meal Timing Guide — Night Shift

Eating on a night shift works best with a plan made before the shift, not decided at 2 AM. Use the example below as a starting point and adjust it to your shift and your appetite.

Example timing for a 7 PM–5 AM shift

WhenWhatNotes
Before shift (about 5–6 PM)Main meal before workEat at home if you can; pack the rest.
Mid-shift break (about 11 PM–1 AM)Packed meal or snackKeep it to what you packed — see page 7.
Later in the shift (about 3 AM)Light snack onlyHeavy food late in the shift can make the last hours harder.
After shift (about 5–6 AM)Small, light snack or nothingEat light, then wind down for sleep.
After waking (afternoon)Main meal of the dayYour “dinner” may land in the afternoon — that is normal on nights.

Caffeine Timing Guide

Caffeine helps most early in the shift and hurts most near sleep. Plan it like a shift tool, not a habit.

A simple rule to try

  • Early shift: caffeine at the start of the shift, if you use it.
  • Mid-shift: one more cup or drink early in the second half, if needed.
  • Last hours: stop caffeine well before your sleep window — many night workers set a cutoff several hours before bed.
  • After shift: water, not caffeine, while you wind down.

After-Shift Reset Checklist

Do this in the same order after every night shift. The point is to switch off work mode before you try to sleep.

On the way home / arriving home

  • Changed out of work clothes 5 min
  • Light snack only, if hungry — nothing heavy 10 min
  • Drank water; no caffeine from here on —

Wind down

  • Shower or wash up 10 min
  • Put tomorrow's sleep window in place: curtains closed, phone on silent 5 min
  • Quick tidy: work bag emptied, lunch bag out, clothes set for next shift 5 min
  • Something calm for 15–30 minutes — reading, stretching, quiet time 15–30 min

Before bed

  • Filled in today's sleep log and energy log (pages 4 and 14)
  • Set an alarm for the end of my sleep window
  • Anything I must not forget tomorrow, written down — not carried in my head
If sleep will not come: get up for a short while and do something quiet in low light, then try again. Do not start errands or bright-screen work. If sleep problems keep happening, talk to a doctor.
