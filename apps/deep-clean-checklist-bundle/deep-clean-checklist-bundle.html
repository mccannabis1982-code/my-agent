Before You Start

Read this page first. A deep clean goes faster when the room is cleared and the supplies are gathered before any scrubbing starts.

1. Pick one room

  • Work top to bottom: ceilings and shelves first, floors last.
  • Finish one room before starting the next — the checklists are built for that.
  • Allow the time estimate on each page; add extra time the first time through a room.

2. Clear the room

  • Remove trash, recycling, and anything that belongs in another room.
  • Put laundry, dishes, and clutter in a basket so surfaces are clear to clean.
  • Open a window or turn on a fan for ventilation while cleaning.

3. Gather supplies

  • Use the supplies list on page 3 — carry a caddy or bucket so you are not walking back and forth.
  • Check product labels and follow them. Never mix cleaning products.
  • Wear gloves when using cleaning products. Wash hands after finishing.

4. Work in order

  • Dust and wipe high surfaces, then mid-level surfaces, then low surfaces and floors.
  • Tick each box as you finish it. Leave anything unfinished for the next pass — the tracker on page 17 keeps it visible.
Tip: Set a timer for the time estimate on each page. If time runs out, stop at a natural break, tick what is done, and pick up where you left off.

Kitchen — Deep Clean

Estimated time: 60–90 minutes  |  Date: ____ / ____ / ________

Clear & prep

  • Clear counters, table, and sink 5 min
  • Empty the trash and recycling; wipe the bins 5 min
  • Load or hand-wash dishes; clear the drying rack 10 min

Surfaces & sink

  • Degrease and wipe the stovetop and range hood 10 min
  • Wipe counters, backsplash, and cabinet fronts 10 min

Appliances — exterior

  • Wipe the outside of the refrigerator, including handles 5 min
  • Wipe the microwave inside and out 5 min
  • Wipe the oven door, dishwasher front, and small appliances 8 min
