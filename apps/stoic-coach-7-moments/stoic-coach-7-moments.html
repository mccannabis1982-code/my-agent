A note on the quotes

Every quotation on this page is copied word for word from a named public-domain translation, with its book or letter and section, so you can check it yourself. Marcus Aurelius, Meditations, translated by George Long (1862) — Project Gutenberg eBook #15877. Epictetus, Enchiridion, translated by George Long. Seneca, Moral Letters to Lucilius, translated by Richard Mott Gummere, Loeb Classical Library (1917–1925). Lines that could not be traced word for word to the texts were left out — including the well-known “You have power over your mind, not outside events,” which is a modern summary of Meditations 8.47, not a sentence Marcus Aurelius wrote.
