4. Check the AI’s Answer

  
Tap each box as you check it. Don’t act on an answer until the important items pass.

  
    
    
  • ✓Verify the date. Is the answer current? Does it mention today’s date or recent events correctly?
    • 
    
  • ✓Check the source link opens. Click it — does it load, and does it actually say what the AI claims?
    • 
    
  • ✓Look for invented numbers. Prices, dates, statistics, and phone numbers are the most common things AIs make up.
    • 
    
  • ✓Cross-check important facts. For anything you’ll act on — a job, a payment, a deadline — confirm it from a second, independent source.
    •
