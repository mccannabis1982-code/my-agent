How to Use This Pack

Inspection aid only — NOT OSHA compliance advice and NOT certification training.

Print the pages you need. Complete one checklist per truck, per shift, before operating. Keep completed forms where your employer directs you to keep them.

Before each shift

  1. Fill in the date, operator name, truck number, and hour-meter reading at the top of the checklist.
  2. Walk around the truck and check each item. Tick OK if it looks and works as expected, Defect if it does not, or N/A if it does not apply to that truck.
  3. Write a short note for any defect. Use the Defect Report Form (pages 9–10) for detail.
  4. If a defect makes the truck unsafe, do not operate it. Tag it out, log it on the Out-of-Service Tag Log (page 11), and report it to your supervisor.
  5. Sign and date the checklist. File it as your employer requires.

What the checklists cover

Both checklists cover: forks, mast and chains, tires, hydraulics, brakes, steering, lights and horn, fluid leaks, overhead guard, and data plate. The electric checklist adds battery, charger cable, and electrical items; the counterbalance checklist adds engine, fuel, and exhaust items where applicable.

Printing tips

  • Print on US Letter paper (8.5 × 11 in), black-and-white, single-sided for filing.
  • Print extra copies of pages 3–8 for daily use, and pages 9–10 for defect reports.
  • Use a pen. Do not erase or alter a completed entry — cross out, initial, and re-write if you must correct it.

Important limits

This pack is an inspection aid only. It is not OSHA compliance advice and not certification training. It does not certify an operator, a truck, or a workplace. Always follow your employer’s procedures, the truck manufacturer’s manual, and applicable regulations for your site.
